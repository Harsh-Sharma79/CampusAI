# CampusAI design direction — Quiet Signal

## Scope boundary
This brief records the product identity for the new managed workspace. The active request is **backend-only**; do not alter frontend pages, layout, styling, copy, or route behavior. The completed frontend was not present in this managed workspace, so its existing implementation remains outside this change.

## Direction
**Quiet Signal** — a premium, intelligent, minimal academic product that feels modern, polished, fast, trustworthy, and quietly futuristic without gimmicks. The user-specified style is the direction; no alternate redesign is proposed.

- **Design movement:** restrained editorial minimalism with clear information hierarchy and practical, high-contrast interface patterns.
- **Core principles:** truth before polish; evidence over invented metrics; keep the learner oriented; make the next action obvious; expose sources and system state.
- **Color philosophy:** deep ink/navy for structure, warm white for calm reading surfaces, and a focused teal signature accent. Use restrained amber only for attention or exam urgency. Avoid decorative gradients and preserve meaningful light/dark palettes.
- **Layout paradigm:** calm modular workspace; primary learning task first, supporting evidence and navigation second. Responsive layouts prioritize legibility and usable study actions.
- **Signature elements:** an open-book form with a small rising signal, source/citation markers, and a subtle topic-path motif. Never imply an unassessed topic has a numeric mastery value.
- **Interaction philosophy:** predictable controls, clear loading/empty/error/success states, retry after real failures, accessible keyboard/focus behavior, and no fabricated activity.
- **Animation:** restrained, short transitions only when they clarify state; honor reduced-motion preferences.
- **Typography system:** modern sans-serif system stack for interface and body text; use tabular numerals for analytics. Avoid externally fetched fonts as a runtime dependency.
- **Brand essence:** the student's grounded academic operating system, not a generic chatbot.
- **Brand voice:** clear, encouraging, precise, and honest about evidence and limitations; no exaggerated outcome claims.
- **Wordmark/logo:** “CampusAI”; a simple open-book mark with an upward learning signal, designed to remain legible at small sizes.
- **Signature brand color:** deep teal `#0F9D9A`, supported by ink `#111827`, paper `#F7F8FC`, and focused amber `#F5A623`.

This document does not authorize or request a frontend redesign in the current backend-only task.
